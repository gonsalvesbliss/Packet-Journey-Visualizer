A timeout is a missing reply from one router, not the end of the journey. The packet tries the red path, gets no reply, then continues on an alternate route (amber) to the next router that answered. Traceroute cannot show the exact alternate path. The amber arc only shows that traffic got through. A timeout has no measurement, so the position of the × is only a marker, not a latency.
